# YG Esports — Yuva Gabbrus

## What is Yuva Gabbrus?

Yuva Gabbrus (YG) is a community-first esports platform built for India's grassroots Valorant scene. It's not about prize money or clout — it's about giving players a real competitive environment to learn, improve, and grow together.

YG exists because most aspiring players in India don't have access to structured competitive play. Ranked queue isn't enough. Scrims are hard to find. Tournaments cost money to enter and only reward the top. That cycle keeps new talent invisible.

We're changing that.

## Why Free Tournaments?

Every YG tournament is **100% free and open**. No entry fees. No paywalls. No hidden costs.

The focus is **training**, not prizes. We believe that consistent, structured competitive experience is what turns good players into great ones — not a ₹5,000 prize pool. When tournaments are free:

- **More teams show up** — especially new ones who can't afford entry fees
- **The focus stays on improvement** — you play to get better, not to chase money
- **The community grows organically** — because everyone is welcome

Occasionally, we partner with content creators to host **sponsored showcase events** — still free to enter, but streamed and promoted to give teams and creators visibility.

## The Community & Esports Vision

YG is built on the idea that esports growth in India starts at the grassroots level:

- **Tier-based progression** — Players and teams climb from Tier 1 to Tier 5 (Esports) based on verified match performance, not just ranked stats
- **Practice scrims daily** — Quick matchmaking for solo queue or 5-stacks at any tier level
- **Community-run, community-owned** — Discord is the heartbeat. Refs, organizers, and players all come from the community
- **Creator partnerships** — We work with content creators to spotlight emerging talent and host special events

This isn't trying to be the next BLAST or VCT. This is the foundation layer — where the next generation of Indian esports talent gets their first real reps.
